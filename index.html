SIH Hackathon — “Chill Can”

Passive Thermal Storage Container to Prevent Milk Wastage Without Electricity

Participated in a hackathon conducted by SIH, organized by the Central Government. In remote rural areas, lack of electricity and delayed transportation cause critical milk wastage for local farmers and dairy producers.

Our team formulated the “Chill Can” — an innovative storage container engineered to preserve milk freshness for extended hours completely without electricity by leveraging passive thermal storage.

⚙ COOLING MECHANISM
PCM (Phase Change Material)

Employs Phase Change Material to absorb thermal energy through latent heat, keeping the internal environment cool for hours without electrical power.

🛡 CONTAINER MATERIAL
HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)

Manufactured with High-Density Polyethylene, ensuring chemical safety and certified suitability for direct food-grade contact with milk.

Key Focus: Tackling energy poverty, rural logistics vulnerabilities, and sustainable refrigeration through passive material design.
SCHEMATIC ID: SIH-CHILL-CAN REV 1.0
Problem: Rural Milk Wastage
Constraint: Zero Electricity Dependency
Core Tech: Phase Change Material (PCM)
Material: HDPE (Milk Contact Safe)
